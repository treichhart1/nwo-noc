=== Northwest Ohio Broadband Customer Cancellation ===
Requires at least: 6.5
Requires PHP: 8.1
Stable tag: 131.0.0
License: GPL-2.0-or-later

Shortcode [nwo_customer_cancellation] and dynamic Gutenberg block embed the existing dedicated HTTPS cancellation request. No applicant data or NOC secrets are stored in WordPress. Disabled until an administrator approves public origin/DNS. TLS and DNS-pinned read-only diagnostics, bounded timeouts, no redirects, no private-destination fallback. Deactivation preserves configuration; uninstall removes only this plugin's options and preserves pages/applications. No live deployment or message authorization.

Cancellation requests never automatically disconnect service or modify Powercode. Policy acknowledgement is not proof of enforceable debt. SMS consent is optional.
